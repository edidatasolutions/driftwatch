# driftwatch 0.1.0

* Initial release.
* Window-level Rasch difficulty estimation (`dw_estimate()`).
* Two-sided CUSUM monitoring with change-point estimation and gradual/abrupt classification (`dw_monitor()`).
* Design-based threshold tuning for a bank-wide false-alarm target (`dw_tune()`).
* Score and pass-rate impact, recommended actions and audit log (`dw_impact()`, `dw_actions()`), and a two-point baseline (`dw_twopoint()`).
