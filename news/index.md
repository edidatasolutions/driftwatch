# Changelog

## driftwatch 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- Window-level Rasch difficulty estimation
  ([`dw_estimate()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/driftwatch/reference/dw_estimate.md)).
- Two-sided CUSUM monitoring with change-point estimation and
  gradual/abrupt classification
  ([`dw_monitor()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/driftwatch/reference/dw_monitor.md)).
- Design-based threshold tuning for a bank-wide false-alarm target
  ([`dw_tune()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/driftwatch/reference/dw_tune.md)).
- Score and pass-rate impact, recommended actions and audit log
  ([`dw_impact()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/driftwatch/reference/dw_impact.md),
  [`dw_actions()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/driftwatch/reference/dw_actions.md)),
  and a two-point baseline
  ([`dw_twopoint()`](https://edidatasolutions.github.io/driftwatch/reference/dw_twopoint.md)).
